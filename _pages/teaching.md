## Book

*Elementos de Computação Matemática com SageMath* (Sociedade Brasileira de Matemática, 2019), co-authored with Marcelo Pedro dos Santos and Ricardo Nunes Machado Júnior.

## Teaching areas

Courses taught include calculus, linear algebra, analytic geometry, real functions, series and ordinary differential equations, and introductory technologies for mathematics education.

## Student supervision

My supervision includes quantum state preparation algorithms and matrix decompositions in quantum computing. I also co-supervise doctoral research at UFPE on quantum machine learning for quantum state preparation. Details are available in my [Lattes curriculum](http://lattes.cnpq.br/2239251029436873).
