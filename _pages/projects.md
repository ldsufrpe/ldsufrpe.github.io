## Scientific software

### PySymmetry

A Sage/Python framework for the symmetry reduction of linear G-equivariant systems, developed with Marcelo P. Santos. The software uses finite-group representation theory to construct symmetry-adapted bases and reduce equivariant operators to block-diagonal form, with symbolic and numerical workflows.

[Source code](https://github.com/ldsufrpe/pysymmetry) · [Documentation](https://ldsufrpe.github.io/pysymmetry/) · [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpc.2026.110455)

### Qclib

A quantum computing library built on Qiskit, with a focus on quantum state preparation. I contribute to the library and to research on algorithms for preparing sparse quantum states.

[Source code](https://github.com/qclib/qclib) · [Double sparse quantum state preparation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-022-03549-y)

### Symmetry reduction for discrete exterior calculus

Research code and data for applying finite-group representation theory to discrete exterior calculus on curved and three-dimensional meshes.

[Code and data](https://github.com/ldsufrpe/dec-equivariance) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25192)
