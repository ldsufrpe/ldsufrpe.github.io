Department of Mathematics

UFRPE · Recife, Brazil

I am a mathematician and professor in the Department of Mathematics at the Federal Rural University of Pernambuco (UFRPE), where I have held a full-time position with exclusive dedication since 2011.

My research brings together discrete geometry, scientific computing, and quantum algorithms. I work on discrete exterior calculus, applied topology, discrete curvature in complex networks, and symmetry methods for mathematical and computational problems. My recent work includes symmetry reduction of linear systems, structure-preserving computations on meshes, and point-group symmetry in variational quantum algorithms.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from UFPE, a master’s degree in Mathematics from UFC, and a teaching degree in Mathematics from UFRPE. I also completed a specialization in Quantum Computing at SENAI CIMATEC through the iNOVA Pós program, with practical work on quantum computers and projects directed toward industry and innovation.

I coordinate the proposal for a new Academic Master’s Program in Applied Mathematics at UFRPE and currently serve as deputy coordinator of Regional 03 of the Brazilian Society of Applied and Computational Mathematics (SBMAC). I also collaborate on quantum computing research at the UFPE Informatics Center.

I co-develop PySymmetry, a Sage/Python framework for symmetry reduction, and contribute to Qclib, a quantum computing library focused on quantum state preparation. I am also a co-author of Elementos de Computação Matemática com SageMath, published by the Brazilian Mathematical Society (SBM).

For my academic background and activities, see my CV and Lattes curriculum. Contact: leon.silva@ufrpe.br.
